## Scope

Correct the applicant-facing projection of the validated baseline while
preserving the profile/document-plan provenance boundary. The projection must
make identity, contacts, employment responsibilities, linked and standalone
projects, grouped skills, education and approved wording readable without
inventing dates or discarding canonical unknowns.

## Acceptance criteria

- Candidate identity is rendered as a bound claim and contacts remain compact.
- Employment headings, responsibility bullets and project placement are clear;
  linked project metadata is not duplicated and standalone projects are distinct.
- Approved skill categories and qualifiers remain visible; internal unknown
  labels are omitted from display only.
- Education, thesis and coursework fields are represented canonically when
  present; date precision and unknown states are preserved.
- Synthetic plans pass independent Poppler/pypdf complete-content QA, identity,
  scope, Unicode, no-hyphenation, A4 and page-break checks.
- Existing provenance, CLI, snapshot/export and isolation regressions remain.
