## F-01 corrective acceptance

Raleway 11 pt Typst layout against the fixed A4 page width after the 20 mm
margins are applied. Each contact scalar is emitted on its own non-breakable
block with an explicit line break. A URL that fits is compared completely by
Poppler and pypdf; a URL that cannot fit is rejected by the renderer with the
bounded diagnostic `contact URL exceeds the fixed A4 20 mm margin and 11 pt
contact width; use an approved shorter URL or omit this contact`. The canonical
URL and extraction normalization are unchanged. This correction does not
regenerate personal outputs and remains awaiting independent review.
