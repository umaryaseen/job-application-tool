# ADR-016: Application sources and submission boundary

- Date: 2026-09-08
- Status: **ACCEPTED FOR PLANNING — NO MVP SUBMISSION**
- Decision owner: supervising engineer; independent review recorded in `../reviews/`.

## Context and research disagreement

R1 764–799, 1165–1190; R2 357, 591–603; R3 727–772.

## Alternatives

Autonomous submissions; supervised prefill; preparation-only pipeline.

## Recommendation / decision

MVP produces reviewed documents for manual submission. Future integrations are independent adapters consuming approved snapshot IDs. Per-source policy defaults deny; explicitly record permitted operations, basis and review date. No live policy facts are hard-coded from old report examples.

## Trade-offs and consequences

Submission adds authenticated actions, terms changes and factual screening declarations. Keeping it separate prevents portal breakage from affecting generation. Even permitted prefill needs review; generic approval of a CV is not approval of salary/authorization form answers.

## Validation and adoption gate

TASK-035 is a later design-only policy/handoff task. No adapter without a separate approved issue and refreshed source terms. No CAPTCHA bypass, mass submission, auto-messaging, or embedding browser sessions in the PDF renderer.

## Reconsideration

Revisit only after M4 reliability and an explicitly authorized source workflow; retain human final submission control unless separately authorized in scope.
