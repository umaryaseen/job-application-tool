synthetic approved ContactUrl containing
`https://example.invalid/profile/this-is-a-very-long-contact-url-with-meaningful-components-and-no-loss`
was rendered through `build_pdf`. The raster is readable and the URL is
visually present, but both Poppler and pypdf insert a line break after
`meaningful-`; the exact expected text therefore fails the existing narrow
normalization. This is in the contact presentation/PDF boundary, not an
extractor-only artifact. Add a focused test in `tests/test_m1_fix004.py` that
constructs a reviewed synthetic ContactUrl and asserts complete Poppler and
pypdf equality, then make the trusted presentation preserve that URL as one
complete extraction token without broad normalization or claim loss.
